## 4. TRAFFIC ACQUISITION STRATEGY

### What This Means for TekDev
You're starting from zero traffic against agencies with 10+ years of compounding SEO. Competing head-on for "web development company India" is a losing battle right now. Instead:

### Phase 1: Foundation (Month 1-2) — GET INDEXED

1. **Fix Technical SEO**
   - Submit sitemap to Google Search Console (pending from SETUP.md)
   - Submit to Bing Webmaster Tools
   - Ensure all pages have unique meta titles + descriptions
   - Add structured data (Organization, LocalBusiness, Service schemas)
   - Fix page speed (target < 2.4s load — above this, conversions drop by 50%+)

2. **Claim Directory Profiles**
   - Clutch.co (FREE listing — this is where agencies get found)
   - DesignRush
   - GoodFirms
   - Google Business Profile
   - LinkedIn Company Page
   - Product Hunt (launch the agency there)

3. **Build Real Case Studies**
   - Each of the 3 live projects needs a dedicated page:
     - Client challenge / problem statement
     - TekDev's approach / tech decisions
     - Before vs after screenshots
     - Measurable results (load time improvement, conversion lift, etc.)
     - Client quote (get real ones)

### Phase 2: Content Engine (Month 2-4) — LONG-TAIL SEO

**Target keywords competitors AREN'T fighting for:**

| Keyword Cluster | Search Volume | Competition | TekDev Fit |
|----------------|--------------|-------------|-----------|
| "chrome extension development company" | Low (100-500/mo) | Very Low | PERFECT — you list this as a service, almost nobody targets it |
| "HRMS portal development India" | Low (200-800/mo) | Low | PERFECT — niche, high intent |
| "fix slow Next.js website" | Med (500-2K/mo) | Low | Great — your bug fixing service |
| "AI chatbot development for startups" | Med (1K-3K/mo) | Medium | Good fit |
| "build MVP fast India" | Low (300-1K/mo) | Low | Perfect positioning match |
| "Shopify to Next.js migration" | Low (100-500/mo) | Very Low | Technical niche |

**Blog content strategy — write what you know:**
- "How We Built [X] in 2 Weeks" (build-log format, extremely shareable)
- "Next.js vs React for Your Startup" (comparison posts rank well)
- "Why Your Website Is Slow and How to Fix It" (attracts bug-fixing leads)
- "Chrome Extension Development: Complete Guide" (own the niche)
- "HRMS Portal Development: Features, Cost, Timeline" (own the niche)

### Phase 3: Founder-Led Visibility (Month 1 onward) — SOCIAL

This is the #1 fastest path to clients. Every successful small agency traces back to a founder who posts online.

1. **Twitter/X** — Post build logs, behind-the-scenes, hot takes on web dev
2. **LinkedIn** — Share case studies, client wins, technical insights
3. **YouTube/Loom** — Record yourself building things (even 2-minute clips)
4. **IndieHackers / Reddit** — Share launches, get feedback
5. **Discord communities** — Be helpful in Next.js, React, Tailwind servers

**The Designjoy lesson:** Brett Williams built a $1M+/year business by being the face of it. People hire people they feel they know. TekDev's founders need to be visible.

### Phase 4: Programmatic SEO (Month 4-6) — SCALE

Create templated landing pages:
- `/services/nextjs-development` — for each tech stack
- `/services/web-development-[city]` — for each target city
- `/hire/nextjs-developer` — Toptal-style talent pages

Each page auto-generates from a data file with unique intro, relevant case studies, and FAQ. This is how Toptal gets millions of visits — thousands of long-tail pages that each get 10-50 visits/month.

## 7. PRICING REALITY CHECK

The current pricing page ($99/$299/Custom tiers with "API calls" and "workflows") looks like a SaaS product, not a dev agency. This confuses visitors.

**What competitors do:**
- **Designjoy:** Flat monthly subscription ($5K-8K/mo), crystal clear
- **Most agencies:** "Get a Quote" / "Book a Call" — no public pricing
- **Budget agencies:** Starting prices ("Websites from $2,000")

**Recommendation:** Either commit to a productized model (like Designjoy — clear pricing, clear scope) or remove the pricing page entirely and funnel everyone to "Book a Call". The current hybrid confuses the brand.

---

## 8. THE BOTTOM LINE

TekDev's **code quality and design are already strong** — the portfolio projects look good, the website itself is well-crafted. The problem is purely **visibility and trust**.

**The fastest path to your first consistent clients:**
1. Founder becomes visible on Twitter/LinkedIn (costs $0, starts working in weeks)
2. Get 5 real reviews on Clutch (costs $0, starts ranking in 1-2 months)
3. Blog targeting niche keywords nobody else owns (costs time, compounds over 3-6 months)
4. Programmatic SEO pages (costs dev time, scales to thousands of indexed pages)

**The hard truth:** Agencies with 300K monthly visits didn't get there overnight. Netguru has been blogging since 2008. But the long-tail keyword strategy means TekDev doesn't need 300K visits — it needs **50-100 high-intent visitors per month** converting at 5-10% to keep a small team busy. That's achievable in 3-6 months with consistent effort.
