## 2. WHAT THIS MEANS (HONEST TAKE)

### The Upside of Low Pricing
- Gets you first clients fast — nobody says no to ₹4K
- Builds portfolio and real testimonials quickly
- Works for local/small businesses (coconut vendors, coaching centres, local shops)
- Low risk for clients = more willing to try a new agency

### The Downside
- **Sustainability:** At ₹4,000 per website, if it takes you 2 days, you're earning ₹2,000/day (₹250/hr). That's below even junior Indian developer hourly rates (₹300–₹500/hr on Upwork)
- **Perception:** Very low prices signal "student work" or "template slapping" — serious clients will skip you
- **Scope creep:** Cheap clients often demand the most revisions and changes
- **Hard to raise later:** Once known as "the ₹4K website guys", it's hard to charge ₹40K later

### The Smart Play: Tiered "Starting From" Pricing

Keep your low entry point to attract first clients, but build a structure that scales up with complexity. This way:
- ₹4,000 is your **lead magnet** (basic landing page)
- Real money comes from **₹15K–₹50K+ projects**
- You're honest — "starting from ₹4,000" is true, but scope determines final price

## 5. HOW TO PRESENT THIS ON THE WEBSITE

### DO NOT use the old SaaS pricing ($99/$299/Custom)
That pricing model (with "API calls" and "workflows") makes zero sense for a dev agency. Remove it completely.

### USE: "Starting From" Cards

Show each service as a card with:
- Service name + icon
- "Starting from ₹X"
- 3–4 bullet points of what's included
- Estimated timeline
- "Get a Quote" button

### Example Card:
```
🌐 Websites & Landing Pages
Starting from ₹4,000

• Responsive design for all devices
• SEO optimized + fast loading
• Contact forms + analytics
• Free deployment assistance

Timeline: 2–20 days
[Get a Quote →]
```

### The Key Rule: Always Show Scope

At these prices, you MUST be clear about what the base price includes. Otherwise clients will expect a ₹4K price for a ₹40K scope. For example:
- ₹4,000 = single-page landing site
- ₹20,000 mobile app = 4–6 screens, basic features only
- ₹15,000 full-stack = MVP with core feature only

## 7. THE GROWTH PATH

### Phase 1: Now (Portfolio Building)
Keep these prices. They'll get you clients FAST. Focus on:
- Delivering excellent quality at these rates
- Getting testimonials + Clutch reviews from every single client
- Building 8–10 portfolio pieces

### Phase 2: 3–6 Months (Price Bump)
Once you have 8–10 projects + reviews:
- Raise base prices by 50–100%
- ₹4,000 landing page → ₹8,000
- ₹15,000 full-stack → ₹30,000
- ₹20,000 mobile → ₹40,000

### Phase 3: 6–12 Months (Agency Rates)
With 15+ projects + strong reviews + blog traffic:
- Move to proper agency pricing
- Websites: ₹25,000–₹1,00,000
- Full-stack: ₹1,00,000–₹5,00,000
- Mobile: ₹1,00,000–₹5,00,000

### The Key Insight
Your current pricing is a **client acquisition strategy**, not a long-term business model. Use it to build the portfolio, then raise prices as credibility grows. Every project at ₹4K that gets you a real review is worth 10x that in future leads.
